Why it's different

Meeting summarizers give you notes. Followthrough gives you a ledger of promises.

01 · Citation gate

No quote, no insight

Every extracted ask must carry a verbatim quote that is checked against the transcript in code. Hallucinated takeaways never reach your queue.

vs. "the client seemed interested in..."
02 · Human gate

The AI drafts, people decide

Nothing reaches a client without a person confirming it. No auto-sent emails, no auto-filed tickets.

vs. bots that email your customers
03 · Event log

Every step is timestamped

An append-only log records each move, so you can measure how fast an ask travels from said to shipped to told, per client and per person.

vs. a summary you read once and lose

Self-host in minutes

Your meetings stay on your server.

One Bun process and a SQLite file. Bring your own Claude key; audio can be transcribed locally with whisper.cpp.

# needs Bun 1.3+
git clone https://github.com/avinashgaurav/followthrough.git
cd followthrough && bun install
echo 'ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=sk-ant-...' >> .env
echo 'PRODUCT_NAME=Acme' >> .env
bun run build:web && bun run start   # http://localhost:4500
